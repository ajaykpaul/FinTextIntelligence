FinTextIntelligence v4.1 - use cases and test cases
===================================================

Last run: 24 Nov 2026, against shared-xlmr.v4.1 (release config), on a MacBook CPU.

Before testing, start the service:

    fintext --config configs/release_v4.1.toml serve

and open http://127.0.0.1:8080/ui. Every test below can be done in the browser.
The API versions use curl, e.g.

    curl -s -X POST http://127.0.0.1:8080/v1/signal \
         -H 'content-type: application/json' \
         -d '{"text": "Net profit rose 18% and the company raised its dividend.", "profile": "trading"}'


Part 1 - use cases
------------------

UC1  Trader checks a headline
     Who:   someone on a trading desk, or an automated strategy.
     How:   paste the headline, profile "trading", press Score.
     Gets:  a signal (BUY / SELL / REVIEW) plus sentiment and confidence. Neutral news
            comes back as a "routine" review, i.e. nothing to trade.

UC2  Compliance screens a social post
     Who:   compliance / moderation team.
     How:   paste the post, profile "compliance".
     Gets:  toxicity (with sub-types and the words that triggered it), scam patterns
            highlighted in the text, and anything risky sent to "Waiting on a person".

UC3  Checking whether a market-moving claim is confirmed
     Who:   analyst.
     How:   paste a claim about a company (merger, guidance, contract...).
     Gets:  provenance state. "pending" means no filing has been seen yet; after the
            window closes it becomes "corroborated" or "uncorroborated".

UC4  Arabic content
     Who:   anyone. Paste Arabic text; it's detected automatically and shown right-to-left.
     Gets:  sentiment and toxicity. Misleading-content check is English only and shows
            as "not run".

UC5  Scoring an article from a link
     How:   paste just the URL. The page is fetched and the article text is scored.
     Note:  needs internet; some publishers block it, in which case the app offers the
            headline taken from the URL instead.

UC6  A person decides on gated items
     Who:   reviewer. Tab "Waiting on a person".
     How:   enter your name, then Accept: buy, Accept: sell, or Reject. Each decision is
            saved with your name and becomes a training label for the next model.

UC7  Weekly quality check
     Who:   reviewer. Tab "Weekly review".
     How:   Draw this week's sample, then mark each flag Correct / Not correct / Unsure.
     Gets:  measured precision per language (target: 90% or more for English).

UC8  Another system calls the API
     How:   POST /v1/score or /v1/signal with JSON. GET /v1/config shows the thresholds
            in use, GET /readyz shows whether the model is loaded.

Notes / known issues
--------------------

1. Misleading-content false positives. T03 (ordinary bad earnings news) and T11 (a
   normal deal announcement) are both marked "needs review" by the misleading model,
   so they go to a person instead of producing a SELL / a held BUY. This is the
   limitation listed in the model card: the English misleading head over-flags
   plain negative or claim-like news. It's safe (a person looks at it) but noisy.

2. T09 also comes back as SELL at 85%. The trading gate only looks at sentiment,
   provenance and the misleading flag, not toxicity, so an abusive post with negative
   tone produces a sell signal. In practice abusive posts shouldn't be fed to the
   trading profile, but it's worth knowing.

3. Running these tests adds items to "Waiting on a person" (the urgent reviews from
   T03, T07, T08, T11). Reject them afterwards if you don't want them hanging around.

4. The automated suite (make test, 625 tests) covers the same ground without the
   model and without a network, and should be run before any change is shipped.
